Basic and Orbital Data
  • Type: Terrestrial (Rocky) Planet
  • Diameter: ~12,742 km
  • Mass: ~5.97 × 10²⁴ kg
  • Surface Gravity: 9.807 m/s²
  • Mean Density: 5.51 g/cm³
  • Escape Velocity: 11.186 km/s
  • Rotation Period: ~23.93 hours (sidereal day)
  • Orbital Period: ~365.25 days (1 Earth year)
  • Average Orbital Speed: ~29.78 km/s
  • Semi-Major Axis (Distance from Sun): ~149.6 million km (1 AU)
  • Orbital Eccentricity: 0.0167
  • Orbital Inclination: 0° (relative to the ecliptic)
  • Number of Moons: 1 (Moon)
  • Atmosphere Composition: ~78% Nitrogen, ~21% Oxygen, trace gases
  • Surface Temperature: ~−88°C to 58°C (average ~15°C)
